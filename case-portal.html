Internal Operations Platform

WeIN Operations Portal

Role: Product Owner + Full-stack EngineerTimeline: 2026—NowStack: Flask, Supabase, JavaScript, TypeScript, n8n

What the product actually does

WeIN OS is the internal command center for moving local businesses from prospect to live marketplace partner. It brings lead follow-up, provider onboarding, offer production, commercial negotiation, contracts, files, team work, and post-signing launch into one operating system.

The operating problem

The provider journey crosses sales, operations, content, and launch. A lead may need repeated follow-up, a menu must be collected and processed, offers need review and approval, commercial terms must be tracked, and a signed partner still needs content, setup, training, and testing before going live. Treating those responsibilities as separate lists made ownership, next actions, and provider state difficult to see.

The real workflow

I translated the team’s actual stages into connected product flows rather than one generic status field.LEADS: NEW → CONTACTING → MEETING → MENU STUDY → OFFERS READY → APPROVAL → PROMOTED
PARTNERS: ONBOARDING → NEGOTIATING → OFFER SENT → ACCEPTED
LAUNCH: SIGNED → CONTENT → OFFER SETUP → TRAINING + TESTING → LIVE

The product surface

A Today queue collects overdue and scheduled follow-ups. Kanban views manage leads, active negotiations, and launch rollout. Provider records connect contacts, location, menu and offer files, offer status, commercial terms, tasks, notes, and discussions. Dedicated views cover deals, providers, offers, analytics, maps, marketing planning, team workload, and team chat, with role-aware behavior for admins, managers, deal breakers, and team members.

How the system connects

The browser application reads operational data from Supabase and uses Flask endpoints for service-side capabilities. Provider intake feeds the automation layer; n8n and AI services extract menus, generate the offer deliverables, and return files and records to the same operational data model. The Portal is therefore not a dashboard placed beside the pipeline—it is the human control layer around it.PORTAL UI ↔ SUPABASE / POSTGRES ↔ FLASK SERVICES ↔ N8N AUTOMATION
CONNECTED RECORDS: LEADS · PROVIDERS · NEGOTIATIONS · OFFERS · FILES · TASKS · CONTRACTS

What I owned

I mapped the operating workflow, defined the product states and role behavior, shaped the information architecture, designed and implemented the interface, connected the data and automation layers, reconciled real provider records, introduced browser smoke tests, and continued iterating from day-to-day operational feedback.

Outcome

The result is a deployed internal product that gives the team one shared place to decide what needs attention today, understand exactly where every lead and provider stands, move work forward, and carry an accepted partner through to launch. It continues to evolve as WeIN’s operating model develops.
