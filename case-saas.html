Medical SaaS

Status: In developmentStage: Private workflow and implementationRole: Product Owner + Engineer

The clinic problem

Many clinics still coordinate core work across phone calls, chat threads, spreadsheets, and disconnected tools. That creates dropped intake details, inconsistent follow-up, poor visibility on patient status, and too much operational load on the front-desk and care team.

What the product is solving

The product is being shaped as an operating system for clinic flow rather than a generic dashboard. The focus is on making intake structured, appointments trackable, follow-up explicit, and team responsibilities visible so the clinic can move patients forward without relying on memory or scattered communication.INTAKE → SCHEDULING → STATUS TRACKING → FOLLOW-UP
ONE CLEAR OPERATIONAL PATH FOR CLINIC STAFF

What I built

I defined the product workflow, mapped the clinic states and handoffs, designed the system architecture, and moved the build into implementation as a private medical SaaS. The work includes the internal product model, role-aware flows, and the application foundation needed to support staff coordination and reliable execution.

Current outcome

This project is still private, but it is no longer positioned as an abstract idea. It is a concrete medical SaaS in active development, with the clinic problem, workflow shape, and product direction already established. Public detail can expand as more of the product becomes safe to disclose.
