# Ahmed Fathy Portfolio - Four Working Worlds

## Positioning

Ahmed is an AI Product Engineer who owns operational products from workflow discovery through architecture, implementation, verification, and release.

## Creative Direction

The portfolio is an explorable sequence of four project worlds rather than a grid of cards.

Reference principles:

- Bruno Simon: navigation feels like entering and exploring a place.
- Dennis Snellenberg: cinematic pacing, oversized type, and confident transitions.
- Stefan Vojnovic: clear engineering evidence inside the visual experience.
- Rauno Freiberg: one meaningful novelty per context instead of constant decoration.

## The Four Main Worlds

### 1. WeIN Offer Pipeline

An animated production line where Intel, Concept, Score, and Verify agents pass a provider offer forward. The final artifact is a grounded offer with every claim checked.

### 2. WeIN Ops Portal

An interactive negotiation board where a real provider deal moves from Negotiating to Signed and automatically creates a launch checklist.

### 3. SaaS in Development

An architectural blueprint whose identity, workflow, intelligence, and operations modules assemble into a working system. It remains explicitly marked as in development.

### 4. Shore Thing

A kinetic beach-sport and DJ-culture poster world with moving typography, a bouncing ball, event stickers, and a day-to-night transformation.

## Supporting Work

Retail Profitability, California Housing, Parkinson's Detection, and the n8n automation library live in a restrained archive so they do not compete with the four flagship projects.

## Quality Rules

- Every flagship project must have a different scenario and motion grammar.
- Motion must explain a workflow, state change, or outcome.
- Each scenario must remain understandable and usable without motion.
- An unfinished product must never be presented as launched.
- Engineering evidence must remain visible beneath the creative direction.
